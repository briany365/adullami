Curriculum

Five axes,
three tracks

Biblical, theological and humanistic lenses open up wisdom and insight, and to these we add practical skill and the footsteps of those who lived this way. What follows is the full course we intend to run once the center opens.

Structure

Why five

Three lenses alone leave no room for practical skill or for real examples. As with a body, five parts have to interlock before there is a single course.

AxisLensThe question it asksWhat remains
RootBiblicalWhat Scripture says about work, money and peopleConviction
FrameTheologicalWhy it is soA frame for discernment
FleshHumanisticWhat a human being isInsight
HandPractical skillWhat you actually do on Monday morningCapability
FootFootstepsWhether anyone truly lived this wayEvidence

The biblical lens reads the text, the theological lens reads the structure and the humanistic lens reads the person. Divided this way, the three lenses come to need one another.

Who it is for

Three tracks

Putting active leaders, emerging leaders and those who help them into one room satisfies none of them. Shared sessions stay shared, but the focus differs.

Main Track

Active Christian business leaders

Judgment, calling and organization. The full passage through all five axes.

12 weeks · 2 retreats · 1 year of accompaniment

Emerging Track

Future leaders and founders

Weighted toward foundations and practical skill. Shares some sessions with the Main Track.

8 weeks · 1 retreat

Companion Track

Pastors and professionals

How to understand and help business leaders, and how to meet a congregant's business life.

4 weeks intensive

There are almost no places where a pastor can learn how to meet a congregant who runs a business. The Companion Track exists for that empty space.

Retreat rhythm

A prayer room belongs
in the timetable, not the floor plan

A space that is built and then left unused is only a room. This is how a day is shaped during the Be'er retreat and the Halak journey.

TimeActivityPlace

07:30Breakfast conversationDining room

The hour at four is required, not optional. For a business leader an hour alone is in practice the hardest discipline of the day, and it is the hour in which the difference of this place is felt in the body.
